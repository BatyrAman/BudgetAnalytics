BudgetAnalytics is a **personal finance tracking and analytics tool**. It helps you record income and expenses, follow budgets and goals, and estimate deposit income from the data you provide.

**Not professional advice.** Nothing shown by BudgetAnalytics is investment, tax, accounting, legal or credit advice. This includes analytics, indicators, the deposit calculator, financial reports and the answers of the AI advisor. These outputs are general and educational. They do not take into account your full financial situation and are not a recommendation to buy, sell, borrow or invest. Consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

**Your data, your responsibility.**

- All figures are calculated from the data you enter or import. If that data is incomplete or wrong, the results will be too.
- Statement import uses column profiles for Kaspi, Halyk and Freedom statements that have **not been verified against real bank exports**. Always review the import preview.
- You remain responsible for checking your records against your bank statements and contracts.

**Calculations are estimates.**

- Deposit calculations follow stated assumptions: nominal rate, ACT/365 day count, capitalization on period anniversaries, rounding to the currency's smallest unit.
- They do not model top-ups, withdrawals, fees, taxes, early termination or bank-specific rules. A bank's actual payout may differ.
- The effective rate (ГЭСВ) is shown as provided by you or the bank and is not used in calculations.
- Cash flow is not the same as an account balance.

**AI-generated text.**

- When an AI provider is enabled, explanations are written by a language model.
- The software checks that every number in the answer comes from figures it calculated, and rejects answers that do not pass.
- The wording, interpretation and recommendations may still be incomplete or inappropriate for your situation.
- The rule-based mode gives general guidance only.
